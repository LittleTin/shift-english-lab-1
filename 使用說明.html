What is included?

Three original tests contain 600 questions: 300 listening and 300 reading. Each test follows the Part 1–7 question counts of 6, 25, 39, 30, 30, 16 and 54. Related questions share a single page, passage and illustration or chart.

The interface and answer choices are in English. Traditional Chinese translations, explanations, option-by-option reasoning, evidence and vocabulary appear on answer-explanation pages after submission, whether the answer was right, wrong or unanswered. Your own notes are preserved in the language you wrote them.

This is independent practice material, not an ETS product or an official TOEIC test. Part 1 uses original AI-generated practice photographs, not official test photographs. Data graphics preserve the original charts and labels. New listening passages use Azure neural voices across 14 English accents, with female and male voices per accent. They are not human recordings. Difficulty and timing have not been officially calibrated.

Practice at your own pace

  • Quick practice: the home page selects approximately 20 reading or listening questions. Complete groups stay together, so the final count can vary.
  • Filtered practice: choose a section, question type, topic, difficulty and target count.
  • Accent practice: choose one of the 14 English accents. A group qualifies only when every listed speaker has that accent. Mixed-accent and unlabelled groups are excluded. If too few questions qualify, the app reports the actual count instead of adding other accents. Accent labels follow the selected synthetic voices, not an official certification.
  • Listening: practice mode allows replay, pause, seeking and speed changes. Moving to another group or leaving the quiz stops and resets the audio.
  • Part 1 and Part 2: answer text is not printed while answering. Choose from the letters after listening. Part 2 also keeps the spoken question hidden. Parts 3 and 4 display all related questions and answer choices.
  • Notes and favorites: use the star to save a question. Practice notes save automatically in this browser.

Formal test mode

A complete test has 100 listening and 100 reading questions. After submission, the result shows raw correct counts and an unofficial score estimate on a 10–990 scale; it is not an ETS score. Listening duration depends on the selected test and is shown before it starts. Reading is timed separately for 75 minutes.

  • The listening timer starts with your first explicit Play. An opening announcement, Part 1 directions and an unscored photograph example play first. Each new Part has spoken directions. Prompts and cues are read in clear American English; scenario dialogues and talks keep all 14 accents. A closing cue introduces Reading. Each group plays once and advances automatically.
  • Formal listening has no replay, seeking, speed adjustment, backward navigation or skipping ahead.
  • Switching tabs pauses only the listening countdown. It continues where it left off when you return. To pause the audio, select Pause Listening. Select Resume Listening to continue the same play from where it stopped, including after a browser interruption or a refresh. Once a group finishes, you cannot replay it.
  • Reading allows navigation among reading groups. You cannot return to the listening section.
  • When a section ends, you cannot add or change answers. A five-second notice precedes the transition to Reading or automatic submission.
  • Legacy saved timed sessions retain their earlier rules and are labelled as legacy sessions rather than being silently converted.

This is a self-study simulation, not an official proctored examination.

Answer first, review afterward

  1. Choose your answers. Practice selections can be changed before submission. Formal listening selections must be completed within the current group's time window.
  2. Submit the session. Correctness, correct answers, translations and explanations stay hidden until submission.
  3. Review the result. The score is correct answers divided by all questions, multiplied by 100. Unanswered questions count as incorrect.
  4. Open an explanation. The results list contains correct, incorrect and unanswered questions. Open an explanation to see the recorded choice and the correct answer. Blank items say Unanswered. In Review Mistakes, the latest incorrect attempt is shown. An older question revision preserves the recorded letter without matching it to newer option text.

For example, 16 correct answers out of 20 gives 80%. This is a practice accuracy score, not an official TOEIC score or a conversion to the 990-point scale. Incorrect answers enter the review list; two consecutive correct attempts remove a question from the pending list, while its history remains available.

Refresh, updates and saved progress

Refreshing in the same browser tab restores the active quiz group and saved answers automatically. It does not restart completed audio or reset the reading timer. If you deliberately return Home before refreshing, you remain on Home and can choose to resume later.

Progress is stored in this browser using IndexedDB, with localStorage as a fallback. If neither works, the app warns that records are temporary. Guest records are not automatically uploaded. If the administrator has opened an account for you, you can choose to sign in and sync completed history.

  • Use a normal browser window. Private-browsing data may disappear when the window closes.
  • Updating the site at the same address in the same browser profile preserves the existing database. Do not clear site data during an update.
  • This first official release does not migrate preview questions or preview listening assets. Within this release, saved answers, notes, favorites, history and unfinished sessions remain available in the same browser. New practice uses the current official questions.
  • Official test IDs are reserved. A conflicting custom definition is not loaded as an official test. Existing saved scores and answers are kept; an unfinished conflicting session cannot be resumed as a different test.
  • Changing the site address, browser profile, device or local file location can change which storage is available. Export a backup first.

Sign in and sync across devices

Open Data & Settings → Account & Sync and sign in with the email and password provided by your administrator. Sign in with the same account on a second device to resume your current practice or timed test as well as see completed history and review mistakes. The status in the top-right corner tells you whether the current tab is signed in. Refreshing that tab normally keeps you signed in; a new tab or browser may ask you to sign in again.

Progress is saved on this device first, then sent in the background. Opening a session with no answers still counts. Return to Practice Home for the Resume Practice or Resume Formal Test card. Keep answering if the connection is slow or offline; another device can only see changes after they sync. If two devices changed the same active progress separately, choose the version to use. Practice recovery can export the saved alternate copy. Guest practice, notes, saved questions and custom pack files are not uploaded by this feature.

Use Change Password after your first sign-in. For a forgotten password, contact the person who opened your account. To bring over records already completed as a guest, choose Copy Guest History and confirm; your original guest copy stays on that device. The app does not move existing records automatically.

Back up and restore

Open Data Management and choose Export Backup. Backups contain answers, mistakes, favorites, notes, incomplete sessions and imported packs. They do not include audio or image files from the website folders.

Importing a backup replaces the current saved records after confirmation. Export your current records first and keep the original backup file. With file://, storage behavior can differ between browsers, so check the storage indicator and keep backups regularly.

Add your own question packs

Import a JSON pack

In Data Management, download the example format. Use a distinct testId and unique question and group IDs, then choose Import JSON Pack. A pack can contain up to 5,000 questions and must be smaller than 20 MiB. Fresh imports cannot overwrite built-in packs.

For additional media, place the files in audio/ or assets/ and use relative paths in the JSON. Importing a question pack does not automatically copy its media files.

Publish a portable pack

In a checkout of the project source, authors can use Python 3.9 or later with tools/add_pack.py to validate JSON, create a JavaScript pack for offline opening and update data/catalog.js. The tool is in the source repository, not included in this web package. Learners do not need Python. See the authoring reference for the pack format and Opus media requirements.

Mobile use

The layout adapts to narrow screens. For reliable mobile access, host the complete folder on a static website and open it in the mobile browser. Upload the audio, images and question data together. Opening local files on a phone differs from opening them on a desktop.

Troubleshooting

  • No correctness shown after choosing an answer: this is intentional. Submit the session to see the score and explanations.
  • No questions match: broaden the filters or turn off the unanswered-only option. Ensure the complete data/ folder is present.
  • Audio will not play: keep the audio/ folder in its original position and click Play yourself. Do not copy only index.html.
  • Saved progress is missing: check the browser profile and exact site address or file location. Import a backup if needed.
  • Publishing the site: upload the complete folder to a static host. Guest practice does not require a server application. Optional account synchronization needs a configured Supabase project.

Privacy and implementation

The app plays pre-generated audio locally and does not call an external speech service during playback. It uses no remote fonts, third-party JavaScript packages or analytics trackers. When signed in, it connects to its configured Supabase project for authentication and completed-session synchronization. Question content and illustrations are original practice material.
