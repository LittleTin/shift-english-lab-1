- **Q095 B, lower-intermediate.** “Cannot use the stairs” maps to step-free
  access, and the room substitution preserves a planned slide show. A loses
  the planned slide show without resolving access; C is irrelevant because
  both rooms share the menu; D contradicts the lift outage. The current
  stem requires little beyond the condition and the room switch. For a more
  defensible middle-band item, use “What would let the group keep its slide
  show while making the lunch accessible without stairs?” /「哪個安排能保留
  投影片簡報，也讓無法走樓梯的人參加午餐？」 B: “Move the lunch and slide show
  to the garden room.” /「把午餐和投影片簡報都改到花園廳。」 This makes the
  new screen setup as well as the access problem relevant.
- **Q096 D intended, required clarification.** The staff can accept the
  cake before 11:00 and the friend expects to arrive at 11:30. “Earlier
  than planned” is not sufficient; 11:10 is earlier but too late. Change
  D to “Have the friend deliver the cake at 10:50.” /「請朋友在上午 10 點
  50 分送蛋糕來。」 (Match other A–C verb forms if retaining a gerund
  options set: “Having the friend deliver the cake at 10:50.”) Rationale:
  「上午 10 點 50 分比原定抵達時間早，也在廚房上午 11 點的收件截止前，
  所以可以交給廚房冷藏。」 Exact 10:50, rather than “before eleven,” still
  asks candidates to apply the heard cutoff and identify which plan works.
  The less realistic original B (“giving the cake to the friend”) may
  optionally become “Having the friend hand the cake to staff at 11:30.” /
  「請朋友在上午 11 點半把蛋糕交給廚房人員。」 
- **Q097 A, intermediate pass.** The stated payer covers only food and the
  restaurant can split by category but not by person. A meets both rules;
  B violates the no-individual-bills rule, C mixes drinks with food paid
  by the office, and D both bills the wrong payer and wants individual food
  payment. If practical, translate “send [a bill] to the office” as
  「把帳單寄給公司」 or equivalent consistent wording, not “collect money
  directly from the office.”
