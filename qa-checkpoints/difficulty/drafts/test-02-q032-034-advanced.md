## Q034 replacement target: independent scheduling and status inference

**EN prompt:** Suppose Friday’s test results meet the requirement. What will most likely be true by the end of Friday?  
**ZH:** 假設星期五的測試結果符合要求，到了星期五下班時，最可能
是什麼狀況？

- **A. The replacement has been approved and the order has already been sent.**  
  替代品已核准，訂單也已發出。  
  **解析：** 星期五收到結果時是下午三點，已錯過採購部中午發單的時段。
- **B. The test results are available, but the team cannot approve the replacement until Monday.**  
  測試結果已出爐，但團隊必須等到星期一才能核准替代品。  
  **解析：** 男子說測量結果合格的話，團隊一收到報告就會核准。受發單時段限制的是發單，
  而非團隊核准。
- **C. The replacement has been approved, but the order will not be sent before Monday.**  
  替代品已核准，但訂單最早要到星期一才會發出。  
  **解析：** 星期五下午三點才拿到結果；即使當下核准，也已錯過星期五中午
  的發單時段。週末不辦公，下一個可發單時段是星期一中午。
- **D. The order was sent at noon, but the replacement was not approved until later.**  
  訂單中午已發出，但替代品稍晚才核准。  
  **解析：** 採購部中午只能發出已核准的訂單，不能在測試結果與核准前
  先發單。

**正解 C，解析同 C。** Requires linking the separate lab/result time,
approval authority, noon dispatch rule and weekend closure. The source
never states the resulting status at Friday close or the next date in the
answer. No production phases or lab-bench names supplied in choices.
Keys remain A/B/C coincidentally; no question points to another key.
